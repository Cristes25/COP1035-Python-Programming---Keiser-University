Lab 4.1: Reading a section code

The subscript operator, slicing and the in operator

Estimated time: about 25 minutes. Reading: Fundamentals of Python: First Programs, 3e (Lambert), Section 4.1, Accessing Characters and Substrings in Strings, pp. 87-91.

You will be able to:

  • Use the subscript operator s[i] to read one character, and convert it with int
  • Extract substrings with slices, counting from the left or from the right end
  • Test for a substring with the in operator, choosing a target that can't match by accident

How to complete it

  1. Open the lab page (4.1.html) from this module. Your work saves automatically in your browser.
